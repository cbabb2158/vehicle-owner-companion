# Vehicle Owner Companion

Vehicle Owner Companion is a personal commercial software project focused on making vehicle ownership information specific, understandable, and actionable.

The first product target is the **2026 Mazda CX-5 Premium Plus**. The core promise is:

> The owner's manual that knows your exact vehicle.

## Current phase

This repository currently contains the product foundation and research structure. It intentionally does not yet include application code, a backend, authentication, subscriptions, or AI integration.

## Product direction

The initial iPhone application will help owners:

- identify controls, indicators, and settings;
- understand features installed on their exact vehicle;
- follow clear, source-grounded procedures;
- find information that is difficult to locate in a conventional manual;
- track maintenance, software updates, accessories, known issues, TSBs, and recalls.

The first implementation is CX-5-only, but the product and data model are intended to support additional models and manufacturers later.

## Repository structure

- `docs/` - product definition, MVP scope, screens, data model, and research backlog
- `research/2026-cx5/` - source material and research notes for the initial vehicle
- `mobile/` - future SwiftUI application

This is a personal project owned by `cbabb2158`. It is completely separate from Optimal Pipeline Solutions. Company repositories, credentials, services, branding, source code, internal documentation, and intellectual property must not be used in this project.

## Research standard

Vehicle guidance must ultimately be traceable to authoritative sources such as Mazda manuals, official service information, recall records, and published technical documentation. Unverified owner reports may inform research questions but must not be presented as established fact.
