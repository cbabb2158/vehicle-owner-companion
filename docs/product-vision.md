# Product Vision

## Promise

**The owner's manual that knows your exact vehicle.**

Vehicle Owner Companion turns scattered, generic, and difficult-to-navigate vehicle information into guidance tailored to the vehicle an owner actually drives.

## Initial customer

The first customer is an owner of a 2026 Mazda CX-5 Premium Plus who is learning a substantially changed vehicle and wants direct answers without searching a long manual, guessing whether a feature applies to the trim, or relying on conflicting forum posts.

## Problem

Modern vehicles combine physical controls, touch interfaces, driver profiles, connected software, safety systems, accessories, and trim-dependent features. Owners often struggle to:

- locate basic information such as the odometer;
- understand unfamiliar settings and terminology;
- determine whether instructions apply to their exact trim and equipment;
- complete procedures that have multiple prerequisites or hidden steps;
- learn what changed in a new model generation;
- distinguish official guidance from speculation.

## Product experience

An owner identifies or selects a vehicle, then asks a question, browses a topic, explores settings, or identifies a control from a photo. The product responds with concise guidance that is specific to the vehicle and grounded in traceable sources.

Answers should make four things obvious:

1. whether the feature applies to the selected vehicle;
2. where the control or setting is located;
3. what the feature does;
4. how to use it safely.

## Long-term capabilities

- VIN-aware equipment and trim guidance
- photo-based control and indicator identification
- step-by-step guided procedures
- maintenance history and reminders
- compatible accessory discovery
- vehicle software-update tracking
- known-issue, TSB, and recall awareness
- source-grounded conversational answers

## Expansion

The content architecture must eventually support multiple model years, trims, markets, models, and manufacturers. Product development begins with the CX-5 only so the team can learn how to produce reliable vehicle-specific guidance before expanding.

## Principles

- Exact vehicle before generic vehicle
- Authoritative sources before confident prose
- Clear procedures before feature descriptions
- Safety and applicability before convenience
- Useful local product before cloud complexity
- Focused CX-5 delivery before multi-brand abstraction

## Success

The product succeeds when an owner can resolve a real vehicle question accurately in minutes and trust both the answer and the evidence behind it.
