# Initial Screens

## Navigation model

The initial prototype uses a garage as the entry point. Selecting a vehicle establishes context for all knowledge and settings content.

## 1. Garage

**Purpose:** Show the owner's saved vehicles and make the active vehicle obvious.

**Content:**

- vehicle image or neutral silhouette;
- year, make, model, and trim;
- owner label;
- masked VIN;
- quick indication of research/content readiness.

**Primary action:** Select a vehicle.

**Prototype data:** Two 2026 Mazda CX-5 Premium Plus vehicles associated with Chris and Jenny, using masked fictional VIN values.

## 2. Vehicle Detail

**Purpose:** Provide the vehicle-specific home screen.

**Content:**

- vehicle identity and masked VIN;
- equipment/applicability summary;
- recently viewed questions;
- knowledge-topic entry point;
- settings-explorer entry point;
- visible indication that prototype content is local.

## 3. Knowledge Topic List

**Purpose:** Let owners browse by the problem they are trying to solve.

**Suggested topics:**

- Displays and indicators
- Comfort and climate
- Driver profiles and personalization
- Controls and convenience
- Garage and HomeLink
- Exterior and accessories
- Software and updates
- Safety and driver assistance
- Maintenance

Each topic shows its available question count and research status.

## 4. Question Detail

**Purpose:** Deliver a concise, trustworthy answer.

**Content order:**

1. question;
2. applicability status;
3. short answer;
4. location or prerequisites;
5. numbered procedure when appropriate;
6. warnings and limitations;
7. source title, version/date, section, and verification status;
8. related questions.

Unverified content must be visibly labeled and must not masquerade as owner guidance.

## 5. Settings Explorer

**Purpose:** Make vehicle settings discoverable even when the owner does not know the manufacturer's exact term.

**Content:**

- search;
- filters by vehicle system and control location;
- setting name and plain-language description;
- menu or physical-control path;
- dependencies and profile behavior;
- default value when verified;
- related questions.

## Accessibility

- Support Dynamic Type.
- Do not use color as the only status indicator.
- Provide descriptive accessibility labels.
- Keep touch targets appropriately sized.
- Use short sections and numbered procedures for scanability.
