# 2026 Mazda CX-5 — Odometer location

## Verification status

Verified for the 2026 U.S.-market CX-5 from Mazda's owner manual on 2026-08-01.

## Owner question

Where is the odometer?

## Applicable vehicle scope

- Model year: 2026
- Model: Mazda CX-5
- Market: U.S.
- Trim: Mazda presents this as a general 2026 CX-5 owner-manual instruction;
  no trim limitation is stated in the section. Confirm on individual vehicles
  during a parked walkthrough if the Mazda Connect home-screen layout differs.

## Verified guidance

The odometer and trip meter are displayed on the Mazda Connect screen rather
than in the instrument-cluster display. From the Mazda Connect home screen,
open **Efficiency**, then select **Efficiency** in the navigation bar to view
the Odometer/Tripmeter information.

Mazda's Center Display Quick Guide also says the Odometer/Tripmeter information
can be checked there and may be added to the Home screen through Home Screen
Personalization.
