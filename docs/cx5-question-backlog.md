# 2026 CX-5 Question Backlog

This backlog captures owner questions to research. Inclusion does not mean an answer or feature claim has been verified.

## Research fields

Each completed item should record:

- exact question and owner intent;
- applicable model year, trim, market, packages, and software version;
- authoritative source and precise section or page;
- direct vehicle observation when useful;
- concise answer;
- prerequisites and procedure branches;
- warnings or limitations;
- verification date.

## Priority questions

### Displays and indicators

- Where is the odometer displayed?
- How does the owner switch among odometer, trip, range, and related information?
- Which display layouts change where this information appears?

### Occupant Comfort

- What does Mazda mean by Occupant Comfort?
- Which climate, seat, steering-wheel, or profile behaviors does it control?
- When does it operate automatically?
- Which settings are remembered per driver?

### Driver personalization

- How are driver profiles created, selected, and edited?
- What vehicle settings are stored in a driver profile?
- How do keys, phones, seats, mirrors, and infotainment profiles interact?
- How can an owner diagnose a profile that does not load as expected?

### HomeLink

- Can HomeLink be programmed without the original handheld transmitter?
- How does the procedure differ for rolling-code and fixed-code openers?
- Where is the garage-opener learn button typically involved?
- What vehicle power state and timeout constraints apply?
- How are existing HomeLink buttons cleared safely?

### Trim and equipment differences

- Why might two vehicles listed as Premium Plus appear identical but have different equipment?
- Which differences can result from market, package, production timing, accessories, or listing errors?
- How can an owner verify installed equipment from the vehicle itself?

### Illuminated sill plates

- Are illuminated sill plates standard, optional, port-installed, dealer-installed, or aftermarket?
- Which part numbers fit the 2026 CX-5?
- How are they powered and activated?
- What installation or warranty considerations apply?

### Software updates

- Which vehicle systems have independently versioned software?
- Where can an owner view each version?
- Which updates are over the air, owner-installed, or dealer-installed?
- How can an owner determine whether an update applies?
- What conditions must be met before starting an update?

### Hidden or poorly explained settings

- Which settings are located outside the central infotainment menus?
- Which settings appear only when parked or under another condition?
- Which settings are tied to a driver profile?
- Which default behaviors commonly surprise new owners?
- Which safety-system settings reset after a drive cycle?
