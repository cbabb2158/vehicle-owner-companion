# Customer Problems

## 1. Basic information is hard to locate

Owners can operate the vehicle but cannot quickly find information such as odometer display, control locations, status indicators, or the correct settings menu.

**Desired outcome:** Give the owner the shortest reliable path to the requested information.

## 2. Vehicle terminology is unfamiliar

Names such as Occupant Comfort or driver personalization may not explain what the system changes, when it operates, or how it interacts with profiles and keys.

**Desired outcome:** Explain features in plain language, then preserve the manufacturer's official name for reference.

## 3. Generic instructions may not apply

Manuals, videos, search results, and forum posts often span multiple trims, markets, packages, model years, or prior vehicle generations.

**Desired outcome:** Show applicability to the selected vehicle before presenting a procedure.

## 4. Procedures hide prerequisites

Tasks such as programming HomeLink may depend on garage-opener type, rolling-code behavior, vehicle power state, or access to a learn button.

**Desired outcome:** Identify prerequisites first and guide the owner through the correct procedure branch.

## 5. Similar vehicles can contain different equipment

Vehicles described with the same trim name may differ because of market, package, production timing, accessories, software, or listing errors.

**Desired outcome:** Explain what evidence can confirm the equipment actually installed.

## 6. Accessories are confused with factory features

Items such as illuminated sill plates may be standard, optional, port-installed, dealer-installed, or aftermarket.

**Desired outcome:** Identify the feature category and provide a reliable compatibility path.

## 7. Software state is unclear

Owners may not know which systems receive updates, whether an update is available, or whether it requires an over-the-air process, dealer service, or another method.

**Desired outcome:** Separate system types and provide version-specific, source-grounded update guidance.

## 8. Useful settings are difficult to discover

Settings may be spread across displays, profile menus, steering-wheel controls, mobile applications, or conditional menus.

**Desired outcome:** Provide a searchable settings map that includes location, purpose, dependencies, and default behavior.

## 9. Trust is expensive

Owners must reconcile the manual, dealer statements, online videos, forums, and memory. Conflicting answers create doubt, especially for safety and maintenance questions.

**Desired outcome:** Attach applicability and source evidence to every substantive answer.
